# Data and scientific attribution

The MIT license covers this repository's software and documentation. It does
not replace the terms or provenance of third-party observations, geometry,
catalog metadata or publications. Retain the source attribution attached to
the selected public examples when redistributing them.

- Model formulation: [Yassin et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-23-3735-2019).
- Weaker-data methodological comparison: [Etienne's study](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjce-2021-0507).
- Approximate reservoir geometry: [Yigzaw et al.](https://doi.org/10.1029/2017WR022040).
- Observation sources and transformations: each public reservoir's
  `observation_provenance.json` and the Gardiner acquisition provenance.

Only the explicitly selected public examples are distributed. Source labels
and publication citations do not certify forcing independence, geometry
accuracy, model suitability or optimizer convergence.
